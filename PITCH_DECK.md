# CrisisOps Agent — Pitch Deck

---

### 1. What Is It?

**CrisisOps** is a Slack bot that helps teams handle emergencies. It turns scattered Slack messages into a structured, accountable response — all without leaving Slack.

---

### 2. The Problem

When something goes wrong, everyone starts posting in Slack. But there is no structure. Facts are spread across channels, nobody knows who owns what, and decisions never get written down.

Teams waste time just figuring out what is happening — when they should be fixing it.

---

### 3. Why Now?

Slack now supports AI agents, real-time message search, and external tool connections (MCP). That makes Slack the perfect place to run a command center, not just a chat room.

CrisisOps is built specifically for this new capability.

---

### 4. What It Does

A command center inside Slack:

- Detects a problem before someone has to explain it
- Opens a structured incident with a commander and a task list
- Generates a clear briefing with evidence
- Finds and matches available resources (generators, staff, volunteers)
- Records every decision with owner and rationale
- Drafts external updates that need a human to approve
- Creates a postmortem when it is all over

---

### 5. The Demo

The full workflow — from detection to postmortem — takes under 3 minutes:

1. Chaos Radar spots the incident
2. One command opens the response
3. Briefing shows what is known
4. Resources get matched and reserved
5. Decisions get logged
6. Postmortem writes itself

---

### 6. How It Works (Technical)

Three layers:

- **Slack** — slash commands, bot mentions, message cards, approval buttons
- **Agent** — intent routing, incident detection, briefing, resource matching, decision logging, approvals, postmortem
- **Tools** — connects to inventory, ticketing, on-call, customer impact, maps, and status update systems

---

### 7. Who It Helps

**Nonprofits, clinics, schools, and public-sector teams** — for disaster relief, public health coordination, and community emergency response.

**Companies** — for outages, security incidents, customer escalations, and executive communications.

Same tool. Both worlds.

---

### 8. Business Value

Faster response = less damage. A structured audit trail = accountability and compliance. Human approval before anything goes external = trust.

CrisisOps is the difference between a team that reacts and a team that commands.

---

### 9. Marketplace Potential

This can be a standalone Slack App with:
- Connectors for Jira, PagerDuty, Salesforce, Zendesk, Statuspage, and more
- Industry-specific versions (hospital, school, logistics, government)
- An incident simulation library for training

---

### 10. Trust and Safety

- Nothing is sent externally without a human approving it
- Every tool call is logged
- Every decision is recorded with evidence
- Fully auditable from first alert to final postmortem

---

### 11. Business Model

- **Free** for small teams and nonprofits
- **Per-workspace** pricing for enterprises
- **Connector packs** for premium integrations

---

### 12. Roadmap

1. Connect to Slack's real-time search API
2. Add production connectors (Jira, PagerDuty, Salesforce, etc.)
3. Build an incident simulation library
4. Publish to the Slack Marketplace

---

### 13. The Close

Slack is already where urgent work happens.

CrisisOps turns that chaos into command.
