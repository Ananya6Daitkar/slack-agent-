# Screenshot And QA Checklist

- Chaos Radar card shows confidence, severity, and three buttons.
- Incident command message shows title, severity, status, and commander.
- Situation brief includes evidence links.
- Resource match shows a high-scoring generator match.
- Decision Ledger entry includes owner, reason, risk, status, and evidence.
- Approved update creates an audit log and MCP tool call.
- Postmortem includes summary, impact, hypothesis, follow-up actions, decisions, and evidence.
- `/demo/state` shows incidents, tasks, decisions, matches, tool calls, and audit logs.
