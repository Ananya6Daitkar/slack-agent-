# CrisisOps — Real Demo Scenario

## Scenario: Storm Causes Power Failure at Rural Clinics

A severe storm damages the local power grid. Three rural health clinics lose electricity. Staff members begin reporting issues across different Slack channels.

---

## Incoming Slack Messages

**#clinic-b**
| Time | User | Message |
|------|------|---------|
| 09:12 | Nurse Sarah | Backup generator failed during power fluctuation. |
| 09:13 | Dr. Patel | Vaccines must stay refrigerated. |
| 09:14 | Operations | Power company estimates restoration in 42 minutes. |

**#volunteers**
| Time | User | Message |
|------|------|---------|
| 09:15 | Alex | Portable generator available at Warehouse B. |
| 09:16 | Jamie | Two volunteers can deliver equipment immediately. |

**#it-support**
| Time | User | Message |
|------|------|---------|
| 09:17 | Monitoring Bot | Clinic B has been offline for 8 minutes. |
| 09:18 | Facilities | Maintenance team is currently assisting Clinic A. |

---

## Step 1 — Chaos Radar Detects the Incident

The AI identifies related messages across multiple channels before anyone calls the incident.

```
Potential Incident Detected

Severity:    High
Confidence:  96%

Evidence
  • Generator failure
  • Power outage
  • Vaccines at risk
  • Multiple affected teams

Recommendation
  Open an incident immediately.
```

Command: `/crisisops simulate`

---

## Step 2 — Open Incident

Command: `/crisisops open incident`

Slack creates a structured incident command center:

```
Incident ID:          INC-2026-041
Status:               Active
Severity:             High
Incident Commander:   Operations Lead
Affected Locations:   Clinic A, Clinic B, Clinic C
```

---

## Step 3 — Situation Brief

Command: `/crisisops brief`

AI-generated summary grounded in real Slack evidence:

```
Situation Summary

Three clinics have lost electrical power following a severe storm.
Clinic B's backup generator failed.
Vaccine refrigeration is at risk.
Estimated utility restoration is 42 minutes.
Two volunteers and one portable generator are available.

Recommended next action:
Deploy the portable generator to Clinic B immediately while
maintenance continues supporting Clinic A.

⚡ Generated by Groq LLaMA-3.3-70b · Evidence-grounded · No hallucinations
```

---

## Step 4 — Resource Matching

Command: `/crisisops match resources`

AI queries the MCP inventory service:

```
Available Resources

🥇  Portable Generator — Warehouse B, 12 km away
    Delivery ETA: 18 minutes
    Available Volunteers: 2
    Priority Match: 98%
```

The Incident Commander approves the recommendation. MCP tool calls logged:
`search_inventory` → `get_location_eta` → `reserve_resource`

---

## Step 5 — Decision Ledger

Command: `/crisisops record decision`

```
Decision:       Deploy Generator to Clinic B
Decision Owner: Operations Lead
Reason:         Prevent vaccine spoilage
Risk:           Clinic A remains dependent on utility restoration
Timestamp:      09:24
Status:         Approved
```

Every decision is permanently recorded for accountability and audit.

---

## Step 6 — Approval Gate

The AI drafts an external status update. Nothing is published automatically — a human must approve before it is sent.

```
Draft Status Update

A power outage is affecting three clinics following a severe storm.
Emergency response teams have deployed backup power to Clinic B.
No patient safety incidents have been reported.
Estimated restoration time is approximately 42 minutes.

[ Approve & Send ]   [ Edit ]   [ Reject ]
```

---

## Step 7 — Postmortem

Command: `/crisisops postmortem`

Auto-generated structured report:

```
Incident Summary
  Storm-related power outage impacted three rural clinics.

Timeline
  09:12  Generator failure reported
  09:18  Incident detected automatically by CrisisOps
  09:20  Incident opened
  09:22  AI situation brief generated
  09:23  Resources matched via MCP
  09:24  Generator deployment approved
  09:42  Backup power restored

Root Cause
  Storm damage caused electrical grid failure.

What Went Well
  • Early incident detection (under 30 seconds)
  • Clear incident ownership established immediately
  • Fast resource matching via MCP inventory
  • Complete decision audit trail generated

Lessons Learned
  • Install redundant generators at critical clinics
  • Expand emergency inventory visibility
  • Conduct quarterly disaster response drills
```

## How to Run This Demo

```bash
npm install
cp .env.example .env
npm run demo
```

Open **http://localhost:3000** and click through the 7 steps in order.

Or in Slack:
```
/crisisops simulate
/crisisops open incident
/crisisops brief
/crisisops match resources
/crisisops record decision
/crisisops postmortem
```
