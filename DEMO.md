## Scenario: Major Earthquake Response Across a City

At 8:42 AM, a 7.2 magnitude earthquake strikes a metropolitan area. Within seconds, hundreds of Slack messages begin appearing across emergency response channels. No single person has the full picture. Different teams report different emergencies simultaneously.

## Step 1 — Chaos Radar

Instead of relying on a human to connect hundreds of messages, CrisisOps automatically detects that these independent reports describe a single city-wide incident.

```
INCIDENT DETECTED

Type:       Major Earthquake
Confidence: 99%
Severity:   Critical

Signals Correlated
  ✓ Hospital failures
  ✓ Power outage
  ✓ Transportation disruption
  ✓ Infrastructure damage
  ✓ Emergency services overload

Recommendation: Open Critical Incident Response

⚡ AI-generated by Groq LLaMA-3.3-70b · Detected in 12 seconds
```

Command: `/crisisops simulate`

## Step 6 — Human Approval Gate

The AI drafts a public statement. Nothing is published automatically — the Incident Commander must approve before release.


## Why This Matters

During major emergencies, responders lose valuable time searching Slack conversations, asking repeated questions, and manually coordinating information across dozens of channels.

CrisisOps transforms thousands of fragmented messages into a single operational picture — helping incident commanders make faster, evidence-based decisions while keeping humans in control of every critical action.

**No new software. No new logins. Just Slack.**

---

## How to Run This Demo

```bash
npm install
cp .env.example .env
npm run demo
```

Open **http://localhost:3000** and click through all 7 steps.

Or run directly in Slack:
```
/crisisops simulate
/crisisops open incident
/crisisops brief
/crisisops match resources
/crisisops record decision
/crisisops postmortem
```
